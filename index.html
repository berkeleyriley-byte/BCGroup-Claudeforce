Compute is becoming a priced, hedgeable commodity

Compute markets are maturing fast. Architect is building a CFTC-regulated exchange for GPU-hour futures, Luxor sells reserved capacity at fixed rates, and Anera Labs describes a forward capacity market for inference. Falling unit prices don’t protect budgets when usage grows several times faster, so planning now includes procurement strategy.
